# Logbook · 18 November 2026

Time zone: America/Lima. Goal: investigate reusing an Estimote Location Beacon F3.3 while preserving evidence of the original firmware and exploring BLE/UART access.

**Result of the day:** sensor reading via BLE advertisements demonstrated from the Mac; reproducible GATT connection with limited access; two UART receive campaigns with zero bytes. No physical UART lock was demonstrated and SWD protection was not yet investigated.

## Identification and references

The owner reports MOD: F3.3, Aug. 22, 2016 and MCU marking `N52832 QFAAB0 1724GK`. The documentary interpretation is nRF52832-QFAA, QFN48, production revision 1, 512 kB flash / 64 kB RAM. A macro photo of our own is still needed to corroborate the transcription. The chip marking date and the design/label date must not be confused.

A reference photo of a board with a layout similar to the unit was provided: logo/antenna at the top, a matrix of eight gold pads on the right and four external contacts at the bottom. The owner confirms that layout. The photo is not presented as a photograph of their own unit. The GPIO diagram identifying GND, Vcc IN (5 V), GPIO0 and GPIO1 was also provided. Historical references associate GPIO0/RX and GPIO1/TX only when UART is enabled.

## Direct capture from the Mac · 14:03–14:08

Python 3.12.12 with Bleak 3.0.1 was installed in `.venv` and CoreBluetooth was used with permission available. The owner confirmed the phone scanner was closed and the beacon was nearby.

- 106 FE9A advertisement events and 12 distinct payloads; RSSI between −77 and −45 dBm.
- Connectable and non-connectable advertisements from the observed identifier were distinguished. The three initial attempts on a non-connectable advertisement timed out; after correcting the selection, three GATT connections completed.
- Two proprietary services and five characteristics exposed by macOS; MTU 23. Characteristic 6c372004… returned `0100` in all three connections; meaning still unknown.
- Two subscriptions were enabled in one session; zero notifications. The subscriptions did not prevent the disconnect. In the next session the client descriptors read `0000`.
- Telemetry subframes A/B, protocol 2: temperature 36.125–37.8125 °C, pressure 1004.12–1004.22 hPa, light 322.56–345.60 lx; first acceleration X/Y/Z ≈ +0.079/−0.031/+1.039 g.
- Battery reported as 316 mV/1 %, normalized magnetic field 0/0/0, and first frame with clock flag. These are protocol fields, without electrical verification or independent calibration; they are not equivalent to the external 5 V and do not demonstrate component failure.

No configuration characteristics were written and no authentication was done. Only scanning, connections, reads and temporary subscriptions were performed. All sessions and scans were terminated. [Acquisition report](../reports/capture-mac-ble-2026-11-18.md), [data and reproduction](../evidence/mac-ble-2026-11-18/README.md).

## UART tests · 14:17–14:20

`/dev/cu.usbserial-113120` was detected, Prolific Technology VID:PID 067b:2303. The owner identifies the adapter as a USB-TTL YP-01 and answers "5v". There is no measurement separating USB power from the TX logic level.

| Campaign | Reported wiring | Settings | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14:17:25–14:18:06 | Original | 8N1; 115200/9600, 8 s each; 57600/38400/19200/230400, 6 s each | 0 bytes in all six windows |
| 14:20:05–14:20:46 | Owner reports swap | Same baud rates and windows | 0 bytes in all six windows |

No bytes or BREAK were transmitted. DTR/RTS were requested inactive and the port was closed after each campaign. It was not confirmed that the adapter's TX was physically disconnected after the swap; stopping writes in software does not remove its idle voltage. During the second campaign eight BLE advertisements from the same identifier were received.

No adapter loopback, oscilloscope/logic analyzer measurement or intentional reset during the windows was done. [UART report and logs](../reports/test-uart-2026-11-18.md).

The owner added that they tried on another laptop with 3.3 V and 5 V without getting it to work. This is a reported result with no logs, schematic, baud rates or clarification of whether power or logic levels changed; it is not interpreted as proof of electrical tolerance or a hardware lock.

## Hypotheses at close

The owner suggests a lock and the need for an unlock instruction that is no longer accessible. The working hypothesis is that UART might not be enabled in the original firmware: the historical precedent required prior configuration and EstiUART. The silence does not confirm it; still possible are absence of spontaneous transmission, contacts/wiring, or an unvalidated adapter.

No universal UART unlock instruction, full shutdown of the cloud service or impossibility of current authenticated configuration was verified. APPROTECT was not measured either. BLE access, Estimote authorization and SWD protection are separate questions.

## Resume point

1. Preserve the firmware: the sensor path via advertisements already works and has raw data.
2. For UART: identify/measure the YP-01 logic level; test loopback isolated from the beacon. Listen to one signal at a time with the adapter's RX and TX disconnected. Do not feed MCU signals with 5 V.
3. If enabling the original UART is the goal: evaluate account, SDK and authorization with current evidence; do not assume an AT console or send arbitrary bytes as an unlock.
4. If custom firmware is the goal: our own oriented photo, unpowered continuity from pads to SWD pins, measured internal VDD and a compatible probe. First identify protection and back up when possible; do not erase blindly.

No flashing, erase, alternative firmware or SWD test. Git remains local, with no commits or remote at the close of this documentation. The project has not been published.
