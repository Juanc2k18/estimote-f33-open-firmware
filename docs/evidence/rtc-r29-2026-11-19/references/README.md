LCC8: 1 SDA, 2 SQW, 3 VSS, 4 NC, 5 VCC, 6 IRQ/OUT, 7 NC, 8 SCL.
Do not apply the QFN16 numbering to the LCC8. The user counts counterclockwise from
the dot they identified; that orientation is kept as a report, without a new
photographic measurement of each contact. The IRQ/OUT route is inferred from the pinout,
not from an alarm generated and read in this test.
